🌆 New York Air Quality Dataset
This repository contains a comprehensive dataset on air quality in New York, covering multiple pollutants, weather conditions, 
And environmental factors over various time periods. The dataset is useful for environmental analysis, public health research,
And predictive modeling to understand air pollution trends in New York.

📌 Features
🌫 Air Pollution Indicators
AQI (Air Quality Index): Daily and hourly AQI measurements.
Particulate Matter (PM2.5, PM10): Fine particulate pollution levels.
Gaseous Pollutants: CO (Carbon Monoxide), NO₂ (Nitrogen Dioxide), SO₂ (Sulfur Dioxide), O₃ (Ozone).
Volatile Organic Compounds (VOC): Presence of harmful organic pollutants.
🌤 Meteorological Factors
Temperature & Humidity: Influence of climate on air pollution.
Wind Speed & Direction: How wind patterns impact pollutant dispersion.
Precipitation & Rainfall: Correlation between rain and air quality improvements.
Seasonal Variations: How air pollution fluctuates across seasons.
🏙 Geographical & Location Data
Monitoring Stations: Data from various air quality monitoring locations in New York City.
Urban vs. Suburban Trends: Comparison of pollution levels across boroughs.
Traffic & Industrial Zones: Impact of vehicle emissions and industrial activity.
